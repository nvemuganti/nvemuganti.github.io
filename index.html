About

25 years in markets. Now I build the systems.

I spent two and a half decades as a financial editor and research manager at Goldman Sachs, UBS, JP Morgan, Reuters, and CRISIL (S&P). I edited equity research, ran a 30-analyst investment research department, and covered live FX markets in real-time for UBS's award-winning client portal across London, Zurich, Singapore, and New York.

Since 2025 I've been applying that domain depth as an engineer — building EdgeQuant, a production suite of 90+ algorithmic trading consoles: Market Profile TPO analysis, options execution, ML signal classifiers, and a 7-agent agentic trading desk where Claude reasons about strategy and a human presses SEND. Concurrently finishing a Masters in Applied Mathematics at Andhra University, specialising in Optimisation and Riemannian Geometry.
